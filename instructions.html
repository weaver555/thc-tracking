❓ How to Use

Simple instructions for customers and riders

👩‍💼 Customer Guide - Track Your Order

1
Place Your Order
Order your food from THC using Loyverse POS system. Your order gets a unique ID like #3-1420
2
Get Tracking Link from Rider
When the rider accepts your order and starts delivery, they'll share a tracking link or your Order ID
3
Open the Tracking Page
Click on the link or go to: Track Your Order button from home page
4
Enter Your Order ID
Type your Order ID (e.g., #3-1420) and click Track
5
See Live Location on Map
The rider's location appears on the map with a red marker. It updates automatically! 📍
6
Contact Rider if Needed
Use the Message Shop or Call Rider buttons to communicate

❓ FAQ for Customers

Q: What if I don't have the tracking link?
A: Just go to the home page and click "Track My Order". Enter your Order ID (ask the shop or rider). It's that simple!
Q: How often does the map update?
A: The map updates automatically every few seconds as the rider moves. Just wait and watch! 🚴
Q: What does "Rider is offline" mean?
A: The rider hasn't started delivering yet or GPS is turned off. Don't worry, they'll go active soon!
Q: Can I share the tracking link with someone else?
A: Yes! Share the link with friends or family so they can see the rider's location too.
Q: Does this work on mobile?
A: Yes! Works perfectly on phones, tablets, and computers. Open the link in any browser.
💡 Pro Tip: Bookmark the tracking page for quick access next time you order!

🚴‍♂️ Rider Guide - Start Delivering

1
Go to Rider Console
Click the "Go Active & Deliver" button from the home page
2
Sign In with Your Email
Use your registered email and password. Make sure your account is created in the system first!
3
See "Welcome" Message
After login, you'll see "Welcome, your@email.com" - you're now logged in! ✓
4
Enter the Order ID
Type the Loyverse order receipt number (e.g., #3-1420) in the field
5
Click "Go Active & Start Tracking"
Your phone will ask for location permission. TAP "ALLOW" so we can track you!
6
Status Changes to "Tracking Active"
You'll see Tracking active for Order #3-1420! - You're live! 📍
7
Customer Gets Tracking Link
Share the tracking URL with customer: https://weaver555.github.io/thc-tracking/track.html?order=#3-1420
8
Deliver the Order
Your location updates automatically as you ride. Customer can see you in real-time! 🚴
9
After Delivery
Click "Sign Out" to stop tracking and clear your status

❓ FAQ for Riders

Q: What if I forget to enable location?
A: Go to your phone settings → Location → Turn ON. The app will ask again, tap ALLOW.
Q: How do I generate a tracking link for the customer?
A: Use the format: https://weaver555.github.io/thc-tracking/track.html?order=ORDER_ID
Replace ORDER_ID with your actual order number.
Q: Can I track multiple orders at once?
A: Currently, one order at a time. Sign out and enter a new order ID to switch.
Q: My location isn't updating. What do I do?
A: ✓ Check location is ON
✓ Make sure you clicked ALLOW for location permission
✓ Refresh the page and sign in again
Q: Does the map drain my battery?
A: It uses GPS, so yes. Keep your phone plugged in or use a power bank during delivery!
⚠️ Important: Always enable GPS tracking before leaving. Customers rely on this to know when you're arriving!
